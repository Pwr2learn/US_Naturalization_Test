# USCIS Naturalization Test (2025 Civics Test) Curriculum Specification

This project now treats the USCIS 2025 Naturalization Civics Test as the source of truth for civics preparation.

## 1. Civics Test Database

- Source: USCIS `128 Civics Questions and Answers (2025 version)`.
- Question bank size: `128` civics questions.
- Standard administration: the officer asks up to `20` questions.
- Standard passing threshold: `12` correct answers.
- Dynamic current-officeholder answers must follow the USCIS updates page at `uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates`.
- Location-based answers still vary by applicant for state senators, representative, governor, and state capital.

## 2. 65/20 Special Consideration

- Applicants age `65+` with `20+` years as a lawful permanent resident may study only the questions marked with an asterisk in the official 2025 bank.
- The officer asks `10` questions from that marked subset.
- Passing threshold: `6` correct answers.
- Applicants may take the civics test in the language of their choice.

## 3. Reading and Writing English Tests

The reading and writing portions remain separate from the civics bank.

- Reading: up to `3` sentences, pass by reading `1` sentence correctly.
- Writing: up to `3` sentences, pass by writing `1` sentence correctly.
- The app should keep these sections simple and practice-oriented.

## 4. Interview Practice

The speaking requirement is still evaluated throughout the naturalization interview rather than as a separate scored test.

- Practice should focus on common officer commands.
- Practice should also cover personal information, travel, taxes, legal history, and oath-related questions.

## 5. Applicability

- USCIS states that applicants who filed Form N-400 on or after `October 20, 2025` take the 2025 civics test.
- Applicants who filed before that date may still be on the 2008 civics test.
- This app's civics section is intentionally aligned to the 2025 civics test.
